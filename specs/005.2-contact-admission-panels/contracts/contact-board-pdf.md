# Contract: contact board and PDF

The Associato contact detail (and edit) shows:

- Panel Consiglio Direttivo with the six board fields
- Bottom panel Anteprima PDF, same viewer as the lead, URL `/api/v1/NonprofitEspocrm/contact/:id/admission-pdf`

After rebuild, already converted Associato contacts have those values filled from their lead when they were empty.
