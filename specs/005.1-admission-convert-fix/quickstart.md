# Quickstart: Associato convert and Italian titles

Local DDEV only. Do not apply on production.

## Prerequisites

- `https://nonprofit-espocrm.ddev.site`
- Interface language Italian for the title check
- An Associato lead with board answers and a visible PDF

## Convert

1. Note the six board answers and open the PDF on the lead.
2. Convert to a contact that stays Associato.
3. On the contact, the Consiglio Direttivo block shows the same answers, and the PDF preview opens.
4. Open the lead again. The PDF preview still opens. There is still one file.

## Not Associato

Convert a volunteer-only lead. The contact has no board block and no admission PDF.

## Titles

In Italian, the contact sections read Volontario / Dipendente and Associato. The user sections read Volontariato and Associato.

Switch the interface to English. Those sections read Volunteer / Employee, Member, and Volunteering.
